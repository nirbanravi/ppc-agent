FAQ

What is SpendSentry?

SpendSentry is an open-source AI PPC agent for Claude Code that audits ad accounts, checks budget pacing, plans budgets and writes client reports across 12 ad platforms, and cannot change an account unless you approve the exact change.

Which ad platforms does it support?

Google Ads, YouTube, Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Microsoft Advertising, Amazon Ads, Reddit Ads, Pinterest Ads, Snapchat Ads, Apple Ads and X Ads, from CSV/XLSX exports or read-only API/MCP access.

Can SpendSentry change my ad account?

Only after you approve. It proposes exact changes with before and after values, a gate checks them against limits such as a 30% budget-change cap, and you approve by typing a one-time code. A hook blocks every other write, including replays and edited requests.

Is SpendSentry free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license and runs locally in Claude Code. The engine does not send your exports anywhere.

What does an audit include?

Scored findings with evidence: conversion tracking breaks, campaigns spending without conversions, CPA and ROAS against target, budget- and rank-limited campaigns, wasted search terms, frequency and creative fatigue signals, spend concentration and pacing. Each finding cites the source file and rows and carries a confidence score.

Do I need to be a developer?

No coding is needed, but you do need Claude Code, which runs in a terminal or code editor. If you can export a report from Google Ads and paste two commands, you can run an audit.
